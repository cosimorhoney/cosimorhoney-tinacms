      Filmmaking has been a long lasting passion of mine, all the way back to
      the home movies made on my dad's old phone. I now see film as a way to
      communicate artistic messages with emotions and atmospheres, whether set
      in otherworldly places or in day to day life. It's also a way for me to
      inspire others to be creative as well. My experience and skills have grown
      rapidly, not only learning the principles that make a good image, but
      gaining an understanding of many aspects of the movie production such as
      story structure, music composition, audio, etc. I apply these skills as
      best I can in my short films posted on my YouTube channel and I am always
      expanding in my education and portfolio through these films. I have
      extensive goals in this industry and I plan to make a strong impact with
      my art and beyond.
